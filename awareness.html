📚 Waste Awareness

Learn how to identify, separate and manage different types of waste responsibly.

🥬

Wet Waste

Biodegradable waste that can naturally decompose.

  • Food leftovers
  • Vegetable peels
  • Fruit waste
  • Garden waste
📦

Dry Waste

Recyclable or reusable materials that should stay dry.

  • Paper
  • Cardboard
  • Glass
  • Metal containers
💻

E-Waste

Electronic items that need special handling.

  • Old phones
  • Computers
  • Batteries
  • Electronic devices

🌱 Smart Waste Management Tips

♻️ Separate Waste

Keep wet, dry and special waste separated before disposal.

🛍️ Reduce Plastic

Prefer reusable bags and containers whenever possible.

🔋 Handle E-Waste Safely

Do not mix batteries and electronic devices with normal waste.
